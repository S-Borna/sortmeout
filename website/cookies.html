Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

Minimal Cookie Usage

SortMeOut uses only strictly necessary cookies to make the website function properly. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. Cookies We Use

We use a minimal set of cookies, all of which are strictly necessary or functional:

Cookie Purpose Duration Type
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie consent choice 1 year Necessary
__cf_bm Cloudflare bot management — protects against automated abuse 30 minutes Necessary
__cfruid Cloudflare rate limiting — prevents abuse of our services Session Necessary

3. Third-Party Cookies

3.1 Stripe (Payment Processing)

When you initiate a purchase, you are redirected to Stripe's checkout page. Stripe may set cookies on their domain to process your payment securely. These cookies are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Cloudflare (Hosting & Security)

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may set strictly necessary cookies for security and performance purposes. These cookies help protect against DDoS attacks and malicious traffic.

4. What We Don't Use

We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or any analytics tracking cookies
  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Any cookies that profile your browsing behavior

5. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, our website uses browser local storage to remember your cookie consent preference. This data stays in your browser and is never sent to our servers.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies

Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
